# Research Standard

## Goal
Build evidence-backed design intelligence without turning DesignLab into a biography archive or an imitation engine.

## Source hierarchy
1. Creator/studio first-party writing or direct interview.
2. Official product/platform material and award documentation.
3. Reputable interviews and case studies.
4. High-quality independent analysis.
5. Background sources only for corroboration.

## Evidence rules
- Every factual claim in a dossier must be supported by a source record.
- Label DesignLab interpretations as inference.
- Do not infer private motives, unpublished methods or sole authorship of collaborative work.
- Prefer repeated evidence over one dramatic anecdote.
- Record contradictions and uncertainty.
- Do not collect source volume for its own sake.

## Copyright handling
Paraphrase principles. Do not copy long passages, proprietary design documentation, screens, assets or signature visual trade dress.

## Maintenance
Add a new source only when it materially changes or strengthens the design model. Version persona packs when a rule changes.
