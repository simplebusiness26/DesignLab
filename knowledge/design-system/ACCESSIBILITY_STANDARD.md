# Accessibility Standard

Baseline: current WCAG 2.2 principles plus platform-native accessibility guidance.

## Required
- readable contrast;
- scalable text;
- adequate touch targets;
- sensible screen-reader order and labels;
- visible focus where relevant;
- reduced-motion support;
- meaning not dependent on colour alone;
- alternatives for drag/gesture-only interactions;
- understandable errors and recovery;
- keyboard-safe layout where keyboards appear;
- no essential information hidden only by hover or haptic feedback.

Accessibility checks happen at concept, prototype and production QA stages.
