## Purpose
Keep the original four-persona full-app tournament intact and run a second, independent whole-app tournament with a new challenger roster. Tournament B does not overwrite, supersede, or modify Tournament A.

## Shared rules
Every challenger receives the same Xplorer Product Truth and whole-app HTML contract. Every candidate must represent the complete app coherently rather than redesigning a single isolated screen. Candidates may change layout, navigation treatment, hierarchy, interaction language, density, visual system and motion philosophy, but may not invent product capabilities, routes, account models or backend behaviour.

## Tournament structure
- Tournament A remains untouched.
- Tournament B is reviewed independently first.
- The user may select whole candidates or individual elements from either tournament.
- A later Champions Round may mix finalists from A and B into new full-app hybrids.
- No candidate becomes authoritative until the user explicitly locks it.

## Review surface
Each challenger is delivered as a standalone, mobile-friendly full-app HTML prototype plus a combined Tournament B review file.
