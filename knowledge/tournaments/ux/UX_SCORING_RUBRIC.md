# UX Scoring Rubric

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Clarity | 15 |
| Usability | 15 |
| Product fit | 15 |
| Mobile/platform ergonomics | 10 |
| Interaction quality | 10 |
| Cognitive load | 10 |
| Accessibility | 10 |
| Distinctiveness | 5 |
| Simplicity | 5 |
| Implementation practicality | 5 |

Scores advise the user; they do not choose the winner.

A lower-scoring concept may still contain the strongest individual idea and can be selected by the user for synthesis.
