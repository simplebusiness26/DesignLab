# UX Tournament Rules

Contestants:
1. Ocho studio / grug lens
2. Jordan Singer-inspired systems lens
3. Loren Brichter-inspired interaction lens
4. Christian Selig-inspired native-app lens

## Fairness
- identical Feature Packet;
- identical Product Truth slice;
- identical brand/platform constraints;
- independent generation;
- no contestant sees another proposal before submission.

## Sequence
1. Four Sonnet persona runs.
2. One reusable prototype builder converts specs into four lightweight prototypes.
3. Human reviews all four.
4. Human preferences are recorded.
5. Opus synthesises **one coherent UX**, not a stitched collage.
6. Human reviews final UX.
7. UX locks only after explicit approval.

## Constraints
- no invented functionality;
- no visual-design tournament yet;
- accessibility required;
- clear trade-offs required.
