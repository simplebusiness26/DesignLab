# Distillation Standard

Research becomes useful only when it changes a decision.

## Conversion pattern
**Evidence:** what a reliable source establishes.  
**Inference:** a transferable design principle suggested by that evidence.  
**Conditional rule:** when the principle should be applied.  
**Guardrail:** when it should not be applied.

Example:
- Evidence: a creator used a direct-manipulation gesture to remove a stop-and-tap interruption.
- Inference: continuous motion can reduce interaction friction.
- Rule: when the desired action naturally follows the user's current gesture, test direct manipulation.
- Guardrail: preserve a discoverable equivalent for important actions.

## Remove
- biography that does not affect design decisions;
- repeated praise;
- superficial style descriptions;
- unsupported psychology;
- one-off product details that do not transfer.

## Keep
- decision priorities;
- trade-off patterns;
- interaction reasoning;
- hierarchy rules;
- platform assumptions;
- accessibility implications;
- failure modes;
- evidence-backed questions.

## Runtime target
Persona packs: 1,500–3,000 words, high density, stable until research materially changes.
