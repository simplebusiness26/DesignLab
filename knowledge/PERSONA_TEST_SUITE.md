# Persona Test Suite

The persona system passes only if the same product truth generates materially different, defensible solutions.

All persona submissions are additionally governed by `PERSONA_PERFECT_10_GATE.md`. That file is a DesignLab system rule, not a trait of any named designer, and it supersedes older persona-local submission thresholds.

## Test fixtures
1. Messaging inbox/thread.
2. Map discovery.
3. Onboarding.
4. Camera/post flow.
5. Feed.
6. Profile.
7. Complex settings.

## Core persona pass criteria
Each persona must:
- preserve identical product truth;
- propose a meaningfully different decision strategy;
- avoid copying known products;
- avoid invented backend capabilities;
- expose trade-offs;
- include accessibility;
- remain implementable;
- avoid caricature.

## Universal submission gate
A persona design is not eligible for tournament review until it passes the global Perfect-10 Gate:
- 10 universal quality areas;
- every area must score exactly 5/5;
- no averaging or compensation;
- every 5 requires concrete design evidence;
- any score below 5 blocks submission;
- after revision, all ten areas are rescored from zero;
- Product Truth, safety/privacy, accessibility and imitation violations can trigger automatic failure regardless of score.

Persona-specific rubrics remain additional diagnostics. They may add stricter or more specialised checks, but they may not lower the global 5/5 submission threshold.

## Failure signals
- four proposals differ only in colours/fonts;
- persona name could be swapped with no change;
- signature gimmicks dominate the concept;
- proposals invent features;
- important actions rely only on hidden gestures;
- the concept works only in a hero screenshot;
- a proposal claims 5/5 without evidence;
- a known weakness is hidden to preserve a perfect score;
- one weak category is averaged away by stronger categories.

See `PERSONA_PERFECT_10_GATE.md` for the full scoring procedure and evidence requirements.
