## Evidence policy
Primary and official sources take precedence. Third-party interviews are used mainly where they contain direct remarks by the creator or detailed contemporary reporting. This file is a research ledger; it is not loaded wholesale at normal runtime.

## Known uncertainty
The pack intentionally avoids pretending to know private methods, tastes or opinions that are not documented publicly. Any broader rule derived from the work is labelled as a DesignLab inference in the dossier.
