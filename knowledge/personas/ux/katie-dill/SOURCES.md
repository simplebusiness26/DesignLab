# Katie Dill — Source Record

## Source policy
This file records the public evidence used to build the Katie Dill design-reasoning lens. The runtime persona must not claim to be Katie Dill or reproduce proprietary screens, brand assets, trade dress, or private/unpublished methods. Public evidence is distilled into general design decision patterns.

## Primary / direct sources

1. **Stripe Sessions 2024 — “Craft and beauty: The business value of form in function”**
   - Publisher: Stripe
   - URL: https://stripe.com/fr/sessions/2024/craft-and-beauty-the-business-value-of-form-in-function
   - Type: first-party talk / transcript
   - Evidence used: quality and craft as business differentiation; care in details; quality as more than visual beauty; user research, data, and expert judgment as complementary signals; quality should be concentrated on the main thing of the product.

2. **High Resolution — Katie Dill (Airbnb)**
   - Publisher: High Resolution
   - URL: https://www.highresolution.design/1-katie-dill
   - Type: direct long-form interview
   - Evidence used: design as how things work rather than only how they look; story-based design; day-in-the-life framing; intentionality, research, iteration, and collaboration; design as a strategic organisational tool.

3. **O’Reilly — “Katie Dill on heading up experience design at Airbnb”**
   - Publisher: O’Reilly
   - URL: https://www.oreilly.com/content/katie-dill-on-heading-up-experience-design-at-airbnb-2/
   - Type: direct interview / podcast notes
   - Evidence used: product, engineering, and design involved from problem definition; storyboards used to inspect the whole journey; design contributing to strategy rather than entering only at the end.

4. **Justinmind — “Experience design and prototyping the Airbnb way: Q&A with Katie Dill”**
   - Publisher: Justinmind
   - URL: https://www.justinmind.com/ux-design/talks/experience-design-and-prototyping-the-airbnb-way-qa-with-katie-dill
   - Type: direct interview
   - Evidence used: design as communication; interfaces, imagery, and conversation building trust; research → low-fidelity prototype → higher-fidelity prototype → experiment / rollout; scale of process should match scale of change; design skills applying to systems and strategy.

5. **Jotform Makers Series — Katie Dill interview**
   - Publisher: Jotform
   - URL: https://www.jotform.com/blog/katie-dill-interview/
   - Type: direct interview
   - Evidence used: research as the cornerstone of decisions; starting with which problems are worth solving; listening to passengers and drivers; testing hypotheses with users and checking the result after shipping.

## Evidence confidence
High confidence:
- research-first problem framing
- journey/storyboarding orientation
- design as whole service, not only screens
- trust and communication between strangers
- cross-functional collaboration from the beginning
- iterative prototyping and validation
- strong quality/craft orientation
- online-to-offline / real-world experience sensitivity

Medium confidence / DesignLab interpretation:
- applying those principles specifically to map-led social discovery
- treating local activity as a service journey rather than a content feed
- prioritising “confidence to act in the real world” as Xplorer’s north-star experience outcome

These Xplorer translations are inferences from the evidence, not claims that Katie Dill has personally prescribed them.
